# Portable session record (private chat only)

The tutor may prepare this **inside the student's private ChatGPT conversation**. It is **not** automatically saved to GitHub, the parent's account, Telegram or PostgreSQL. Do not place real student names, essays or results in this public template file.

## Session record

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (student may omit)
- **Session type:** Diagnostic / Lesson / Writing / Review
- **Topic or source:** ...
- **Time spent (optional):** ...
- **Completed tasks:** ...
- **Reading evidence:** claim, supporting detail and reasoning — summary only
- **Writing evidence:** first draft completed? revised? which rubric dimension improved?
- **Observed skill families / provisional codes:** ...
- **Independence:** independent / hints / substantial scaffolding
- **Confidence in evaluation:** low / moderate / high; why
- **Retention:** not checked / check scheduled / retained / revisit
- **One specific strength:** ...
- **One next step:** ...
- **Suggested next session:** ...
- **Limitations:** what was not assessed or could not be verified

## Parent-facing Russian summary (only when student requests)

- Сегодня: [что сделано].
- Подтверждённые навыки: [конкретные наблюдения, не официальный балл].
- Что пока сложно: [одна-две темы].
- Самостоятельность: [потребовались ли подсказки].
- Следующий шаг: [задание на завтра].
- Достоверность: [что оценивала AI, а что ещё нужно проверить человеку].

## How to continue in another conversation

Student can copy a **short summary without personal identifiers** into the next private session and say:

> Continue my Cambridge Stage 9 English learning from this session record. Ask one question at a time and do not assume anything not written here.

Do not promise that a plugin can access prior chats, long-term memory, private databases or school systems without an actual authorized connection.

# Curriculum reference — Cambridge Lower Secondary English 0861, Stage 9

**Scope:** first-language English (0861), not English as a Second Language (0876).

## What is verified

A Cambridge-authored **Curriculum Framework version 2.0 (2021)** has been located in a publicly readable third-party-hosted PDF. Its Stage 9 section includes **67 coded learning objectives**, grouped as:

| Strand | Objective identifiers | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Reading | `9Rv`, `9Rg`, `9Rs`, `9Ri`, `9Ra` | 25 |
| Writing | `9Ww`, `9Wv`, `9Wg`, `9Ws`, `9Wc`, `9Wp` | 25 |
| Speaking & Listening | `9SLm`, `9SLs`, `9SLg`, `9SLp`, `9SLr` | 17 |

The public [Stage 9 objective code index](STAGE9_OBJECTIVES.csv) includes the identifiers and **our original, broad skill classifications**; it does **not** reproduce Cambridge's copyrighted objective descriptions.

The historical framework gives a teaching-time guide of **4–5 hours per week / 120–150 hours per stage**. Five 50–60-minute sessions are a reasonable *pilot planning target*, not a mandatory Cambridge daily schedule.

## What is not verified

- The precise revision currently used by the student's school, its Year 9 scheme of work and Year 10 pathway.
- Any official Cambridge mark scheme for the pilot questions or essays.
- The right to copy full Cambridge objective descriptions, past papers or school-only materials into a public GitHub repository.
- Whether third-party Oak National Academy lessons have appropriate reuse rights and match each Cambridge objective.

## How the plugin should use this index

1. Teach broad Stage 9 **skill families** through original/legally usable texts.
2. When linking a lesson to an objective code, label the mapping **provisional** until checked against the school's official materials.
3. Require actual learner evidence: short answers with quotations, essays, oral responses and later retention checks. A completed lesson does **not** automatically mean mastery.
4. Mark skills that require live human discussion, speaking performance or handwriting as **not fully assessable** by text chat alone.
5. Never claim that ten pilot lessons complete the entire Stage 9 course.
